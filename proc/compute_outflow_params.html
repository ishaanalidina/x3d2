public subroutine compute_outflow_params(self, out_vel, flow_rate_diff)

Convective outflow number and inlet/outlet flow-rate imbalance for a case with an x inflow and a convective x outflow (the X_FACE form of field_set_face_from_field). Three slice reductions on the device and two batched MPI_Allreduces; no D2H copy of the full field. Assumes x is not decomposed, as the FFT Poisson solver requires. Without flow_rate_diff only the outflow number is computed.
