public subroutine create_plans_110(self, mesh, fft_n, fft_n_loc, fw_plan_type, bw_plan_type)

R2C with Z-transpose, on (nz, nx, ny). A single rank keeps the plain cuFFT path; on multiple ranks cuFFTMp distributes the transform, and the physical Z-slab is redistributed into its Y-slab input through an MPI_Alltoall (see redistribute_110_to_yslab).
