public subroutine prepare_probe(ctx)

Decides the FFT probe once per run and runs it. The small-grid probe gives the context and the heap. The full-grid probe follows when the mode allows and the card has room for the memory the plans need. The decision is in ctx%probe_plan and ctx%probe_reason (see ensure_probe in the device).
