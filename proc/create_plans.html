public subroutine create_plans(self, mesh, fft_n, fft_n_loc, fw_plan_type, bw_plan_type)

Standard R2C/C2R plans on the grid permuted into the spectral layout, with cuFFTMp fallback. This serves the 000, 010 and 100 cases. fft_n and fft_n_loc are the plan dimensions from cuda_fft_plan_setup.
